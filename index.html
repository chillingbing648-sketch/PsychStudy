    HOW TO SUPPORT A FRIEND IN DISTRESS · 5-STEP CLINICAL PEER GUIDE
    01
    Listen Actively Without Judgement

    Give them your complete, unhurried attention. Do not dismiss their pain with platitudes ("you'll get over it", "look on the bright side") or compare their distress to others.

    02
    Ask Directly and Compassionately

    Clinical evidence demonstrates that asking directly ("Are you thinking about suicide?") does not plant the thought in someone's head; instead, it offers immense relief and permission to speak openly.

    03
    Prioritize Immediate Environmental Safety

    If you believe they are in acute danger, do not leave them alone. Gently restrict access to potentially lethal means and stay with them in a calm, safe environment.

    04
    Facilitate Professional Connection

    Offer to sit with them while calling Tele-MANAS (14416) or help them schedule an appointment with a university counselor, clinical psychologist, or psychiatrist.

    05
    Maintain Ongoing Continuity and Check In

    Follow up the next day and throughout the following weeks. Consistent, caring connection significantly reduces social isolation and fosters resilience.
